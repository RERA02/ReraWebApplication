Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund Policy

  1. Refund of Registration Fee

    (a) In case a promoter applies for withdrawal of application of the Project before the expiry of 30 days, registration fee to the extent of 5% paid or ₹25,000 (whichever is higher) shall be retained by the Authority and the remaining amount shall be refunded.

    Similarly, in case of rejection of an application for:

    • Registration of a Project
    • Registration of a Real Estate Agent
    • Extension of Project Registration
    • Renewal of Real Estate Agent Registration
    • Map Revision of a Project

    Registration fee to the extent of 5% shall be retained as administrative charges and the remaining amount shall be refunded.

  2. Applicants must submit refund requests through the "Refund Request" module available on the Rajasthan RERA Website/App. Approved refund requests will be processed within 10–15 working days from the date of approval. Refunds will be credited to the bank account provided in the refund request.
  3. No refund request submitted through any other mode, including chargeback requests raised with banks or payment service providers shall be entertained.
  4. Out of multiple payments, one amount will be adjusted against the successful Transaction, and all other amounts will be refunded. However, payment gateway charges, credit card charges, debit card charges, net banking charges, and any other platform/service charges will not be refunded.
  5. Non-Refundable Fees

    No refund shall be admissible if the following applications are rejected:

    • Change of RERA Bank Account
    • QPR
    • APR
    • Promoter Profile Modification
    • Project Profile Modification
    • Bank Account Closure
    • Form-X
    • Complaint
    • Encumbrance Update
    • Completion Certificate
    • Occupancy Certificate
    • Post Approval

Cancellation Policy

  1. Consumers cannot submit cancellation requests for funds successfully paid against a successful transaction.
  2. Once a payment has been successfully processed, it is considered final and non-reversible. Consumers/Citizens should carefully verify payment details before completing the transaction.
  3. After successful payment, confirmation via Email/SMS/Payment Receipt will be displayed or sent. This shall serve as proof of payment and should be retained for future reference.

Chargeback Policy

  1. Applicants shall not initiate any chargeback request with their bank, card issuer, payment gateway or payment service provider after successful completion of a transaction. Any payment issues shall be resolved through the refund policy.
  2. Raising a chargeback shall be treated as a malafide (illegal) activity. The department reserves the right to initiate recovery proceedings against the Consumer/Citizen.
  3. Consumers should follow the refund policy for payment discrepancies. Refunds shall be processed according to the established refund policy.

Important Notes

  • All applications, registrations, complaints, refund requests and other matters submitted through the Rajasthan RERA Portal shall be governed by the provisions of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, Rajasthan Real Estate Rules, 2017 and other applicable laws.
  • Any dispute arising out of services provided through the Rajasthan RERA Portal shall be subject to the jurisdiction of competent courts and authorities in Rajasthan, India.
  • Rajasthan RERA reserves the right to amend, modify or withdraw any policy, guideline, procedure, fee structure or service without prior notice.
  • Applicants should carefully read and understand all applicable provisions, instructions, terms and conditions before submitting any application, registration, complaint or refund request.
  • Submission of any application or request does not automatically confer any right, approval, registration, refund or relief. All requests are subject to scrutiny and approval by the Authority.
